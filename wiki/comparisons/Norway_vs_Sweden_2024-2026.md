# Comparison: Norway vs. Sweden (2024–2026)

## Summary Table

| Metric (2026 Forecast) | Norway | Sweden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Policy Rate** | 4.2% | 1.8% |
| **Unemployment** | 2.1% | 8.4% |
| **Wage Growth** | 4.5% | 3.4% |
| **GDP Growth** | 1.4% | 2.2% |
| **Inflation (Target 2%)** | 3.3% | 1.5% |

## Key Insights
1. **The Great Divergence**: The most striking difference is the interest rate path. Norway's rates are more than **double** Sweden's (4.2% vs 1.8% forecast for 2026).
2. **Labor Market Divide**: Norway maintains nearly "full employment" (2.1% unemployment), while Sweden struggles with a much higher rate (8.4%).
3. **Growth Swap**: While Norway was more resilient in 2023-2024, Sweden is expected to grow faster in 2026-2027 as its early rate cuts stimulate the economy.
