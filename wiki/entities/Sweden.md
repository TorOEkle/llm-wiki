# Sweden: Economic Development

## Overview
Sweden has a highly developed, export-oriented economy. Monetary policy is managed by **Sveriges Riksbank**, with an inflation target of 2.0% (measured by KPIF).

## Current Economic Status (as of March 2026)
- **Policy Rate (Styrränta)**: 1.75% (as of March 2026).
- **Inflation (KPIF)**: 2.6% (2025 average), forecast **1.5%** for 2026.
- **GDP Growth**: 1.8% (2025 actual), forecast **2.2%** for 2026.
- **Unemployment**: 8.4% (2026 forecast).

## Key Trends
- **Rapid Rate Cuts**: The Riksbank lowered rates aggressively in 2025, reaching 1.75% by March 2026.
- **Unemployment Peak**: Unemployment peaked around 8.8% in 2025, significantly higher than in Norway.
- **Growth Recovery**: GDP growth is expected to pick up pace in 2026-2027 (2.2%-2.3%) as interest rates remain low.
