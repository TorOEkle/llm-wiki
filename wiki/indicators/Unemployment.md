# Indicator: Unemployment

## Definition
Labor market tightness measured as a percentage of the labor force.

## Historical Unemployment Rates

| Year | Norway (NAV) | Sweden (AKU) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2023** | 1.8% | 7.7% |
| **2024** | 2.0% | 8.4% |
| **2025** | 2.1% | 8.8% |
| **2026 (F)** | 2.1% | 8.4% |
| **2027 (F)** | 2.3% | 8.0% |
| **2028 (F)** | 2.3% | 7.6% |

## Comparison/Analysis
- **Structural Difference**: Sweden has a significantly higher baseline unemployment rate (often 3-4x higher than Norway).
- **Correlation**: Both labor markets cooled in 2024-2025 due to the delayed effects of high interest rates on consumption and investment.
- **Recovery**: Both countries expect the labor market to start stabilizing or improving slightly by 2027-2028 as the economies recover.
