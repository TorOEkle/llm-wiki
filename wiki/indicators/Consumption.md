# Indicator: Consumption (Household)

## Definition
Household consumption (Privat konsum/Hushållens konsumtion) measures the total spending by households on goods and services. It is the largest component of GDP in both countries.

## Historical & Forecasted Growth (Annual % Change)

| Year | Norway | Sweden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2023** | -0.1% | -2.5% |
| **2024 (P)** | 1.1% | 2.0% (Mars 24 forecast) |
| **2025 (F)** | 2.8% | 1.6% (Mars 26 forecast) |
| **2026 (F)** | 1.9% | 2.4% (Mars 26 forecast) |
| **2027 (F)** | 1.7% | 2.6% (Mars 26 forecast) |
| **2028 (F)** | 1.7% | - |

## Comparison/Analysis
- **The 2023 Dip**: Sweden saw a much sharper contraction in consumption (-2.5%) compared to Norway (-0.1%) as high inflation and interest rates hit Swedish households harder and earlier.
- **Recovery Paths**: Both countries expect a recovery in consumption as real wages start rising again. Norway's recovery is projected to be very strong in 2025 (2.8%) before stabilizing. Sweden's consumption is expected to pick up pace in 2026-2027 as interest rates remain low.
