# Indicator: Interest Rates

## Definition
The policy rate (Styringsrente in Norway, Styrränta in Sweden) is the primary tool for monetary policy.

## Historical Policy Rates (Annual Averages)

| Year | Norway (Styringsrente) | Sweden (Styrränta) | Difference (NO-SE) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2023** | 4.50% | 3.50% | +1.00% |
| **2024** | 4.50% | 3.60% | +0.90% |
| **2025** | 4.30% | 2.10% | +2.20% |
| **2026 (F)** | 4.20% | 1.80% | +2.40% |
| **2027 (F)** | 4.20% | 1.90% | +2.30% |
| **2028 (F)** | 3.70% | 2.10% | +1.60% |

## Comparison/Analysis
- **Divergence**: While both countries had high rates in 2023-2024, their paths diverged sharply in 2025. Sweden's Riksbank cut rates much more aggressively (reaching ~2% average in 2025) than Norway's Norges Bank (~4.3%).
- **Policy Stance**: Norway has maintained a more restrictive stance to support its currency (NOK) and combat more persistent inflation.
- **Normalization**: Both countries expect to settle into a "neutral" rate environment by 2028, though Norway is expected to remain slightly higher.
